# Netherlands jobs expansion: academic, research and public sector

Checked: **2 October 2026**. This audit documents **29 distinct listings** integrated into `data.json` after the original 19; each is a specific employer vacancy with a current application route and a future closing date. Availability can change before the deadline.

## Inclusion and mapping rules

- Primary employer vacancy pages or employer-hosted recruitment systems only. Search engines were used for discovery; aggregator claims were not accepted over employer evidence.
- `cities` contains advertised work locations, not every consortium partner or an assumed corporate headquarters. Den Haag is normalized to The Hague. Pins represent city centres, not office addresses.
- Explicit “apply before” dates are conservatively stored as the previous calendar day; inclusive “no later than” / “up to and including” dates are stored unchanged. The exact wording is noted below.
- Applied AI, trustworthiness in deployment, efficient hardware and open foundation-model development are distinguished from direct frontier-AI safety. These jobs are opportunities, not impact endorsements.
- No visa sponsorship or remote work is inferred. Eligibility constraints with material consequences are retained in the cards.
