# Netherlands AI jobs: industry expansion audit

Checked: **2 October 2026**. This audit documents 20 new records integrated into `data.json`; the original records are retained.

## Inclusion method

Opened employer websites or their own ATS role pages, checked explicit Dutch job location, role-specific duties, application availability where exposed, and closing dates. Search-result snippets were discovery material; removed or expired roles were excluded. Each record links directly to a role, not a general careers index. Generic form success text in Recruitee templates was not treated as an application actually being submitted. No applications were sent.

All 20 records are **Broader AI**. Some involve evaluation, reliability or privacy, but the roles do not claim a dedicated frontier-AI-safety remit. This deliberately complements the pre-existing SAIN and research/governance records.

## Limits

This is a manually checked snapshot, not a live vacancy feed. A working role page can outlive hiring; users should check the linked employer before investing in an application. The two unknown-compensation records avoid treating unspecified pay as explicit evidence. No sponsorship claim is derived from employer headquarters, relocation benefits, or an internationally diverse workforce.

## Local validation

Parsed JSON; 20 unique new IDs and URLs; no overlap with existing IDs; all records contain required fields and allowed categories/relevance/status values; the only declared deadline is not before the check date. No main dataset or index file edited.
