## Classification and omissions

- Relevance labels are editorial classifications of the advertised work, not ratings of social impact. AI alignment is within the NLP professor’s advertised interests; the broad role is classed as broader AI, rather than a dedicated safety position. Formal verification, interpretable biology, energy modelling and OpenML are classed as broader AI, not automatically frontier-safety jobs.
- Volunteer opportunities are explicit and filterable. PhDs are employment-based research positions; “entry level” means career stage, not an absence of degree or technical requirements.
- Work mode is unspecified unless the employer makes it clear. No vacancy has been labelled as visa-sponsored by inference from an institution’s international character.
- Excluded passed-deadline results: RIVM’s AI/infectious-disease PhD (28 September), Radboudumc’s agentic prostate-cancer PhD (1 October), UvA’s flood-scenario PhD (17 September). Unverified broker/aggregator-only vacancies were not included.
- Discovery links are separate navigation aids, not extra vacancies. No jobs were copied from 80,000 Hours; the source is an optional global discovery board.
